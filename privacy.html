Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026Operator: XfinitGaming

This Policy explains how XfinitGaming (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information through Stream.Me’s Discord creator tools, platform monitoring, optional account connections, and this website.

Public visibility matters. Creator alerts, portfolios, LIVE trackers, Discovery posts, and recaps can appear in Discord channels. Recaps may name supporters whose public stream activity was observed, including people who never registered with Stream.Me.

1. Information we handle

Discord and creator information

We handle Discord user and server IDs, relevant channel, role and message IDs, display names, avatar references, membership, roles, permissions, and presence information used by creator features. We store submitted platform links, creator applications and review decisions, notification preferences, portfolio bios and appearance selections, and relevant timestamps.

Monitoring records can include profile metadata, stream titles, categories or games, image references, LIVE status, observed start/end times, content links, link-health results, delivery and role-action history, and recap activity.

Observed stream activity

For supported TikTok LIVE sessions, the separate LIVE integration can observe viewer and like counts, supporter identifiers, usernames and display names, likes, gifts, shares, follows, joins, and fan-club status or level. Activity and summary information may be cached locally. TikTok chat events are ignored by the current activity collector.

For YouTube, the bot may read live-chat API responses to derive participant channel IDs, names, chat counts, and support-event counts. The inspected collector does not save chat-message text, but participant and aggregated activity can be retained for recaps. Do not interpret “no saved chat text” as no chat processing.

Displayed results depend on available platform data. These observations can include public participants who have not linked an account to the bot.

Support and diagnostics

We process information you provide in support requests. Operational records can contain errors, profile handles, identifiers, and delivery status. Browser-based profile discovery may save diagnostic screenshots when a lookup fails; those images may contain the profile content visible during that lookup.

2. Optional TikTok account connection and public-video Discovery

Availability: official TikTok account connection and public-video Discovery are limited to authorized test accounts while production approval is pending. They are not publicly available as of the date above. These practices apply when the features are enabled for your account and you choose to authorize them.

The connection requests user.info.basic and user.info.profile and reads an app-scoped account identifier (open_id) and current username. We associate these with your Discord user/server, registered URL, connection status, and timestamps. Access and refresh tokens are encrypted in local storage; the encryption key is kept separately in the service environment. We do not collect your TikTok password.

After authorization on TikTok, you must confirm the displayed account in Discord. The service periodically checks the authorized profile, normally about every 15 minutes, and updates a username only for the same connected identifier. Tokens may be refreshed automatically. Revoked or expired access may require reconnection.

The browser flow uses a necessary, secure, HTTP-only session cookie valid for up to ten minutes and clears it during callback handling. One-time connection tokens are stored as hashes. Temporary attempts and unconfirmed connection records expire and are pruned during later storage operations. The connection does not request video publishing, private videos or messages, or account-management permissions.

Read-only public-video access

For accounts where official video Discovery is enabled and the creator grants video.list, Stream.Me retrieves public video IDs, publication times, titles, descriptions, cover-image references, and share links using TikTok’s video-list API. These details may be displayed in the configured Discord Discovery channel. No private videos are requested, and this feature does not download or re-upload full TikTok videos. TikTok LIVE monitoring is separate.

The bot stores public video metadata and delivery/baseline records associated with the Discord user and an app/account-derived hash to prevent repeats and retry failed deliveries. Checks are periodic; the initial check establishes a baseline without reposting old uploads. Disabling New Content alerts suppresses posts, not checks or baseline updates.

3. Why information is used

  • Check creator access, review applications, and save requested profile links.
  • Detect supported LIVE streams and new content, update roles and dashboards, and deliver enabled notifications.
  • Build portfolios and observed-activity recaps.
  • Keep an optionally authorized TikTok account linked when its username changes.
  • Prevent duplicate or excessive alerts, troubleshoot failures, handle requests, and protect the service from misuse.

4. Visibility and service providers

Members with access to configured Discord channels may see creator profiles, avatars, stream/content metadata, portfolio text, presence status, and recaps, including selected supporter names. Private bot controls are intended for the person using them; relevant server administrators can access application, registration, and service-management information. Public posts can be copied by viewers.

Discord and supported content platforms process requests under their own policies. Stream.Me uses APIs and browser-based discovery for services including TikTok, Twitch, YouTube, Instagram, Kick, and X where configured. X discovery can use Nitter. The unofficial TikTok LIVE connector may use an Eulerstream signing service; it is separate from official Login Kit. Profile discovery may use operator-configured platform session cookies.

This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting, network, and any configured HTTPS proxy providers may process IP addresses and request metadata. Platform image links or Discord-posted media can also involve their hosting providers. Credentials are not included in public creator cards or this website.

Relevant information may be disclosed to providers needed to operate the requested features, at your direction, or as reasonably required for valid legal obligations and protection against abuse. Following a third-party link subjects you to that service’s practices.

5. Storage, retention, and removal

Creator records and operational state are stored locally using the service’s database and supporting files. They persist across bot restarts. Historical recaps, delivery history, applications, preferences, portfolios, diagnostic files, and backups may remain after a current registration is removed. There is no single automatic time-based deletion schedule covering all these records.

Removing a platform registration removes its current registration and associated active state; a TikTok registration removal also clears its local optional authorization. Disconnecting only the optional TikTok connection removes local credentials, attempts remote revocation, and stops official video checks, but keeps the public registration. Historical Discord posts and public-video deduplication records may remain. A full deletion request must cover those records and relevant backups; disconnecting alone is not a full-data deletion request. Remote revocation may fail if TikTok is unavailable; you can revoke permission directly in TikTok as well.

The bot checks membership when cleaning departed members’ registrations. If the member remains in another server shared with the bot, registration cleanup may not occur. Leaving a server, disabling notifications, or removing the bot is not a full-data deletion request.

Request deletion using the contact below. We will verify that the request concerns your information, handle it under applicable requirements, and explain any relevant exceptions or backup limitations. Some records may need to be retained for security, disputes, or legal obligations. Existing Discord messages and copies held by others may require separate removal. Do not assume that unlinking automatically removes public posts or backups.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

  • Use Link Platform or Update Platform to correct saved profiles; remove a registration to stop future monitoring for that link.
  • Use Alert Preferences to control posts. Disabling posts does not disable monitoring, the LIVE tracker, or streaming-role updates. Turning off New Content alerts also does not stop official public-video checks or baseline updates.
  • Where enabled, use Disconnect TikTok to remove its optional account connection and stop official video checks, or revoke permission in TikTok. Remove the public registration separately to stop other monitoring. Request deletion separately for historical records and relevant backups.
  • Edit your portfolio to change published information. Ask staff or support to remove existing cards or messages where needed.
  • Request access, correction, deletion, or raise an objection or privacy concern through our contact below. These requests are also available to stream participants mentioned in recaps.

Include your Discord user ID or public platform handle and enough context to locate the relevant records. We may ask you to demonstrate control of the account, but never to provide a password, verification code, or access token. Rights and response obligations depend on your location.

7. This website and cookies

These public pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, login forms, or application-set cookies. GitHub and other providers may maintain technical logs under their own policies. The optional TikTok connection runs separately and uses the short-lived security cookie described above. This website does not receive your bot connection tokens.

8. Security and international processing

Access checks, owner confirmation, short-lived connection attempts, and encryption of optional OAuth tokens reduce risk, but no system is perfectly secure. Not all service records are encrypted. Avoid sharing secrets or sensitive personal information in bios, forms, or stream activity.

Discord, platform, hosting, and network providers may process information in countries different from yours. The service is not intended for people below the applicable Discord or connected-platform minimum age. Contact us if you believe information from an ineligible child has been collected.

9. Changes

We will revise this Policy when practices change and provide additional notice when appropriate or required. The date above identifies the current version.

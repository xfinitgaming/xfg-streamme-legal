Creator guide

Last updated: September 25, 2026Operator: XfinitGaming

Stream.Me’s working creator tools live inside Discord. This website explains the flow; it does not sign you in or collect your platform credentials.

Get startedManage profilesPreferencesTikTok connectionHelp & removal

1. Start in the Creator Portal

  1. Join a participating Discord server and open its Stream.Me Creator Portal.
  2. Select Apply for Creator and provide at least one supported creator profile you are entitled to link. Stream.Me checks available activity and sends the application for administrator review.
  3. After approval, open My Status to see your Creator Center, linked profiles, role status, and recent delivery information.

Applying or connecting TikTok does not automatically grant creator approval. Ask server staff if your application is pending.

2. Keep your profiles current

Use Link Platform to add a profile and Update Platform to correct one. TikTok, Twitch, YouTube, and Instagram are among the supported profiles; LIVE and content features differ by platform.

Use your actual profile URL, not an individual video URL. If a platform username changes before automatic account connection is enabled, update the saved link manually. Link-only registrations that stay invalid may receive reminders and eventually be removed; other platform registrations are not removed by that process.

3. Go LIVE or publish new content

Stream.Me checks registered profiles periodically. When a LIVE is detected, it can post an alert, update the tracker, and assign the configured streaming role. New supported posts or videos can appear in Discovery. When a LIVE ends, an enabled recap can update the alert with activity the bot observed.

Brief interruptions, reconnect handling, anti-spam limits, private profiles, network errors, or platform restrictions can affect timing and delivery. Missing values mean the information was unavailable, not necessarily zero. Stream.Me cannot make a Discord user appear online.

4. Choose which notifications are sent

Open My Status → Creator Center → Alert Preferences to control LIVE Alerts, New Content, Recaps, and Night Sharing. Eligible Ladies or Fellas Night sharing also depends on the server’s roles, schedule, and LIVE-alert setting.

Turning off notifications does not stop monitoring. The LIVE tracker and streaming-role checks can continue. To stop checking a platform, remove its registration or contact support.

5. Build your creator portfolio

Open My Portfolio in the Creator Portal. Edit your bio, choose a preset in Theme Studio, preview your card, then publish or update it. Published cards and their profile links are visible wherever the server posts them. Do not include private information in your bio.

Optional TikTok account connection and public-video Discovery

Status: test-only while production approval is pending. Official TikTok account connection and public-video Discovery are available only to authorized test accounts, not publicly. When enabled for your account, the private Platform Center offers Connect TikTok. Authorize on TikTok’s own website, return to Discord, select Check connection, and confirm the displayed account.

Connect TikTok requests basic/profile permissions to read an app-scoped account ID and current username, plus read-only access to your public videos. It does not request permission to publish to TikTok, read private videos or messages, or manage your TikTok account. LIVE monitoring is separate.

Once connected, Stream.Me checks for the same account’s username changes about every 15 minutes. It cannot automatically identify an already-renamed account from an old manual link alone: correct that link once before the first connection. Revoked or expired authorization may require reconnection.

For official public-video Discovery, Stream.Me checks periodically rather than receiving an instant upload alert. The initial check saves a starting point without reposting old uploads. Later new public videos may appear in the server’s configured Discovery channel when New Content alerts are enabled. Turning off New Content alerts suppresses posts, not checks or baseline updates. This feature does not download or re-upload full TikTok videos.

Disconnect TikTok removes the stored connection credentials, attempts revocation with TikTok, and stops official video checks. The manually registered public profile remains; remove that registration as well if you want other monitoring stopped. Prior Discord posts and deduplication records may remain; disconnecting is not a full-data deletion request. See the privacy choices.

Help, corrections, and removal

If a post is missing, check My Status, your alert preferences, profile visibility, and whether the saved link is current. Contact staff for persistent errors. Do not register another person’s account to bypass ownership checks.

Unlinking stops future checks for that registration, but does not erase all previous Discord posts or history. Contact us for an access, correction, or deletion request, including your Discord user ID or relevant public platform handle.

Contact XfinitGaming at xfinitgaming00@gmail.com or through our Discord support server. Include “Stream.Me” in your request. Never send passwords, verification codes, access tokens, or identity documents.
